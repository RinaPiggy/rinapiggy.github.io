I’m a fourth-year PhD researcher in **TU Delft’s Department of Transport & Planning**, specialising in **applied machine learning for urban transportation and city logistics**.

I develop probabilistic forecasting and reinforcement learning methods to support decisions in shared mobility, multimodal travel and last-mile delivery. My work combines transformer and graph-based learning with an understanding of transport networks, fleet operations and uncertainty in real-world demand and supply.

Within the European **SUM project**, I led modelling and coordinated delivery of a shared-mobility forecasting service integrated into **RET’s live travel-planning app** in Rotterdam. My work with industry partners spans data access, model integration, app functionality and user evaluation. Earlier research with **Just Eat Takeaway** connected demand forecasting with courier management and delivery operations.

I hold an MSc in Computational Science from the University of Amsterdam and a BSc in Econometrics and Operations Research from Erasmus University Rotterdam.
